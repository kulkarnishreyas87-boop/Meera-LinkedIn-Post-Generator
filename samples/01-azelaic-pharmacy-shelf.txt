Pharmacy in Bandra yesterday. Azelaic acid 10% "gel" next to a 20% cream from another brand, the 10% one priced higher. Pharmacist told a customer the 10% is "gentler so better for daily use". Azelaic acid barely dissolves in water - at 10% in a watery gel most of it is probably suspended, not dissolved, and the base decides how much actually reaches skin. Percentage on the box says almost nothing about delivered dose here. Same point as niacinamide post but a different mechanism (solubility not pH). Worth doing?
